## Industrial AI research demo — Vision2DCS

[![NEXUS Vision2DCS offline P&ID and instrument-tag engineering workspace](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/senanurcetin/Vision2DCS/main/docs/assets/nexus-workspace-desktop.png)](https://github.com/senanurcetin/Vision2DCS)

**[Vision2DCS](https://github.com/senanurcetin/Vision2DCS)** is an active, **non-commercial NEXUS engineering research demo**: an offline human-reviewed P&ID tag workflow, React Flow signal-topology visualization, illustrative Siemens PCS 7 / ABB 800xA exports, local alarm education and seeded image degradation.

**Measured boundary:** the real Tesseract 5.3.4 OCR experiment on **10 synthetic drawings** produced tag-detection precision **52.94%**, recall **19.15%** and F1 **28.13%**. Those are **not Gemini results** and do not establish generalization to independently drafted plant P&IDs or certified vendor imports. The public demo performs no paid inference or OT commands.

[Interactive offline demo](https://vision2dcs-nexus-demo.vercel.app/) · [Science and evidence](https://github.com/senanurcetin/Vision2DCS/blob/main/docs/DEMO_EVIDENCE_MATRIX.md) · [CircleCI quality check #356](https://circleci.com/gh/senanurcetin/Vision2DCS/356)
