## Interpretation and limits

The positional fast path provides a pronounced speedup for **wide, many-label** frames; for **tall, narrow** frames it is approximately neutral. Peak whole-process RSS stayed broadly similar. Two-run medians have limited statistical power, and these results do not guarantee performance on other hardware, workloads or NumPy/pandas versions. The previous 46.81s versus 0.63s measurement came from a different harness and should **not** be directly compared to these results. No benchmark-derived merged-PR count, developer-program eligibility, published package, or external adoption is implied.
